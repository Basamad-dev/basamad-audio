Low-latency GSC beamforming

Switch between raw microphone, delay-and-sum and GSC output while the audio keeps playing. Then shorten the frame shift to see what latency costs and what it keeps.

Press Play, then change any setting while the audio runs.

What to listen for

  • Raw versus GSC: how far the interferer drops while the target stays intact.
  • Frame shift from 64 down to 8 samples: the output delay shrinks. Listen for changes in noise reduction and artifacts.
  • The removed signal: it should contain the interferer and almost none of the target. Target leakage shows up here first.
  • The moving interferer: how quickly the adaptive stage re-converges.

How the audio was made

Renders come from simulated rooms with a known array geometry, reverberation time and source layout, processed offline. Each file is time-aligned, loudness-matched and looped, so switching settings never restarts playback.

Replace the placeholder signals by saving files as audio/gsc/<scenario>_<processing>_<frameshift>_<listen>.wav. The raw and delay-and-sum options ignore frame shift, so use x in its place, for example static_raw_x_out.wav.
