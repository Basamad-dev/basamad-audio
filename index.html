07 — About & Contact

About Basamad

Basamad is an independent audio DSP engineering consultancy focused on algorithm development, adaptive signal processing, microphone-array techniques, echo control and speech-related audio systems.

The consulting approach connects signal-processing fundamentals with practical software engineering: clarify the acoustic problem, develop and test an algorithmic approach, then support implementation, integration and evaluation.

Machine learning is considered as a complementary tool when it is appropriate to the problem; the core emphasis remains on robust, explainable audio signal processing and engineering.
