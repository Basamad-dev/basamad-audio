Separating talkers by direction

Several people speak at once. Pick a talker and hear what the array recovers when it steers toward that direction.

Press Play, then change any setting while the audio runs.

Measured for this setting

What to listen for

  • Mix versus separated output: the other talkers should sink well below the selected one.
  • Talkers closer in angle are harder to separate. Compare the 2-talker and 4-talker scenes.
  • Steering to different talkers: the quality can differ with each position.
  • The removed signal shows what was suppressed to get the selected talker.

How the audio was made

Each scene mixes separately recorded talkers rendered at fixed angles in a simulated room. The talkers are known individually, so separation is scored against the clean source.

Save files as audio/separation/<talkers>_<method>_<talker>_<listen>.wav. The unprocessed mix ignores the talker choice, so use x in its place.
