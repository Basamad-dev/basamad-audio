Acoustic echo cancellation

Hear how each canceller handles the hard moments: the far-end talker interrupting, and the room changing mid-call.

Press Play, then change any setting while the audio runs.

Measured for this setting

What to listen for

  • Convergence: how long the echo stays audible at the start of each file.
  • Double-talk: whether the near-end voice is clipped or distorted while the far end is active.
  • The echo-path change: how fast each method recovers.
  • Residual suppressor on versus off: remaining echo against added artifacts.

How the audio was made

A far-end speech signal is played through a simulated loudspeaker and room response, then mixed with near-end speech. Every method receives the same signals, and the metrics are computed offline from the known echo and near-end components.

Save files as audio/aec/<scenario>_<method>_<rt60>_<listen>.wav. The unprocessed option ignores the RT60 setting, so use x in its place.
